01Master project

Predicting a model's final quality from a partial training run

Problem
Training large models is expensive. Teams want to stop weak runs early, but early loss curves are noisy and easy to misread.
Built
A pipeline that forecasts final validation loss from a fraction of a curve, using two foundation-model families (ifBO/FT-PFN and TabPFN-style predictors) and 14 strategies for choosing which past runs to condition on.
Result
Found which strategies transfer from small to large scales, and showed the two families differ sharply in how stable their uncertainty is — judged by interval scores and coverage, not accuracy alone.
PythonPyTorchTabPFNpandasSlurmcalibration
02AutoML

Searching for an architecture and its hyperparameters at once

Problem
Architecture and hyperparameters are usually tuned separately, so a promising architecture gets discarded because it was tested with bad settings.
Built
A joint NAS + HPO search over convolutional models on Fashion-MNIST, with a shared budget and a comparison of search strategies against random search.
Result
A working end-to-end AutoML pipeline, plus a practical read on where a limited search budget actually pays off.
PythonPyTorchNASHPO
03Deep learning

Does switching optimizers mid-training actually help?

Problem
Adam converges fast early, SGD often generalises better late. Practitioners debate switching with little controlled evidence.
Built
A benchmark that switches optimizers at different points during training and tracks convergence speed and final accuracy across settings.
Result
Quantified when switching helps and when it costs accuracy; presented as a research poster.
PythonPyTorchbenchmarking
